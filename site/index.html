Check a project name before you commit to it

Searches domains, GitHub, npm, PyPI, crates.io, RubyGems, Packagist, NuGet, Homebrew, Docker Hub, Wikidata and Hacker News for the name and for names that look or sound like it.

Use lowercase letters (a–z) and numbers (0–9) only. No spaces, hyphens or other special characters.

This is not legal advice: trademarks aren't checked. Before you commit to a name, search the trademark databases for your market.
